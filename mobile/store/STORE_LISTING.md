## Product boundary

Scorecaster provides sports analysis, AI explanations, risk controls and paper tracking. It does not accept deposits, process withdrawals, store payment cards or bank information, connect to bookmaker accounts, hold a real-money balance, place bets or redirect users to place bets.

### Description

Scorecaster is a sports-analysis and paper-tracking workspace for people who want to understand probabilities, market prices and risk.

Use Scorecaster to:

- review supported sports and leagues
- compare market odds and implied probability
- inspect model probability, estimated edge and expected value
- see trust scores and PLAY, CAUTION or SKIP guidance
- record paper picks using a virtual bankroll
- set virtual stake and open-exposure limits
- settle paper picks and review profit, ROI and closing-line value
- export your account data and permanently delete your account in the app

Scorecaster does not accept deposits, process withdrawals, hold money, store payment information, connect to bookmaker accounts or place real-money bets. All bankrolls, stakes and results inside the app are simulated paper-tracking values.

Model outputs are estimates, not guarantees. Sports outcomes are uncertain and users must independently evaluate every decision.

### Full description

Use Scorecaster to compare odds, understand implied probability, review model estimates and track simulated paper picks.

The application includes supported league filters, model probability, estimated edge and expected value, trust scores, PLAY / CAUTION / SKIP guidance, a virtual bankroll, stake and exposure limits, paper-pick settlement, profit, ROI, CLV, data export and in-app account deletion.

Scorecaster never accepts deposits, processes withdrawals, holds money, stores payment details, connects to bookmaker credentials, places real-money bets or provides a button that sends users to place a bet. All bankroll and stake values are simulations used only for analysis and paper tracking.

Model outputs are estimates and cannot guarantee profit or predict sports results with certainty.
