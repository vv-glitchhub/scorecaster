# Age-rating and gambling-content draft

Answer the store questionnaires truthfully based on the final binary and the wording shown in each console.

## Current Scorecaster content

Scorecaster displays sports events, market odds, probability estimates, paper stakes, a virtual bankroll, paper results and risk guidance. It does not accept money, award money, execute wagers, connect to a bookmaker, sell lottery entries or provide casino games.

## Recommended disclosure position

- Real-money gambling: No
- Simulated gambling game: No. Scorecaster does not simulate a roulette, slot, card or sportsbook game loop.
- Gambling references or themes: Yes, when the questionnaire treats odds, paper bets, bankroll terminology or betting analysis as gambling-related references.
- Contests or sweepstakes: No
- Purchasable loot boxes: No
- User-generated public content: No in the MVP
- Unrestricted web access: No. The app opens only product policy/support pages from fixed buttons.
- Location sharing: No
- Mature sexual, violent or drug content: No beyond ordinary sports-event names supplied by data providers

## Reviewer explanation

All bankrolls and stakes are virtual paper-tracking values. The app has no deposit, withdrawal, cash prize, bet execution or bookmaker redirect capability.

Do not choose a lower age rating by hiding the presence of odds or paper-betting terminology. Use the rating produced by the current store questionnaire and local market requirements.
